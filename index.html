O06 — Restarting services in the right order

Quando um serviço fica bloqueado e nada nele mudou, reinicie apenas esse contentor com docker restart.

  • One stuck service where nothing changed: `docker restart <container>`; a changed image, setting or secret: a redeploy (`./deploy.sh <tenant>`); never `docker compose restart` or a hand-typed `docker compose up`; save the logs and the exit state first; wait for `(healthy)`; check the platform; record it.
  • The ten-step start order for the whole platform, why it exists (compose `depends_on` and health checks), and that a slow service is not a failed one.
  • After a server reboot most of the platform stays down (no restart policy) and OpenBao comes back sealed.

O09 — Renewing the HTTPS certificate

Os cidadãos chegam à plataforma pelo nome, em HTTPS.

  • The certificate model: a purchased wildcard, signed by an intermediate that the root signed; nginx must send the certificate first and the intermediate second.
  • How to check the chain and the expiry from outside (`openssl s_client -showcerts`, `-checkend`), and what a missing intermediate looks like (return code 21, `curl` error) — the November 2026 lesson.
  • The renewal steps: check the two files, build the fullchain in order, verify offline, match the key, back up, install, `nginx -t`, reload, check again, roll back if needed; then the Android check and the records.

O10 — Kafka lag: find it and clear it

Atraso do Kafka: encontrá-lo e resolvê-lo.

  • What lag is (messages written but not yet processed), and why it matters while every service looks green.
  • How to read it with `rpk`: cluster health, the group list, the total-lag loop, `rpk group describe` (TOTAL-LAG, LAG per partition, members).
  • What each pattern means and what L4 does: restart the consumer with `docker restart` and watch the lag drain; escalate one-partition lag; free disk; never move offsets alone.

O12 — Auto-scaling

O escalonamento automático acrescenta ou retira cópias de um serviço conforme a carga muda, e só existe em Kubernetes.

  • Auto-scaling exists only on Kubernetes; Mozambique runs a single server, where each service is one container and nothing scales by itself.
  • What the Helm charts actually switch on (HPA only for Novu, ingress-nginx autoscaler off, DIGIT at one replica, VPA off).
  • What to watch instead on a single server (disk, memory, Kafka lag, database connections) and the two ways to add capacity.
